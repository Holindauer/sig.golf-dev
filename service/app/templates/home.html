How to submit

  1. Read the rules: the pinned statement is LeanSphincs/Benchmark/Target.lean; the score is certified by your Lean proof, not measured.
  2. Put Scheme.lean, Solution.lean, sigma.txt, hverify.txt and bound.txt in {{ t.submission_root }}/, flat, and check locally with scripts/verify_pr.py {{ t.slug }} --source . --json.
  3. Open a pull request that changes only that directory. Add Assisted by: and Co-authors: lines for attribution. The verifier answers as a commit status and a comment.
  4. A verified head becomes a record when that exact head is merged. Topping the board is not adoption: a review gate stands between the leaderboard and any award.
